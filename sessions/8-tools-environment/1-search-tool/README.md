Here is a summary of some of the latest significant research papers in 2024 on AI applications in biological sciences, based on recent high-impact publications:

1. "Accurate structure prediction of biomolecular interactions with AlphaFold 3" (Nature)
   - This paper presents AlphaFold 3, an advanced AI system accurately predicting biomolecular interaction structures, enhancing understanding of molecular biology.

2. "scGPT: toward building a foundation model for single-cell multi-omics using generative AI" (Nature Methods)
   - Introduces scGPT, an AI foundation model designed to analyze single-cell multi-omics data, improving insights into cellular heterogeneity and function.
   - URL: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-024-02201-0

3. "EvoAI enables extreme compression and reconstruction of the protein sequence space" (Nature Methods)
   - EvoAI is a computational tool leveraging AI to understand the relationship between protein sequences and functions, aiding protein design and synthetic biology.
   - URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-024-02504-2

4. "Contextual AI models for context-specific prediction in biology" (Nature Methods)
   - Describes PINNACLE, a graph-based AI model integrating 3D protein structure with single-cell genomics to predict cell-type specific protein interactions.
   - URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-024-02342-2

5. "Programmable biology through artificial intelligence: from nucleic acids to proteins to cells" (Nature Methods)
   - Explores AI-enabled computational tools that facilitate biological programming and molecular/cellular engineering across various scales.
   - URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-024-02338-y

6. "Nucleotide Transformer: building and evaluating robust foundation models for human genomics" (Nature Methods)
   - Introduces a nucleotide-level transformer model for predicting molecular phenotypes directly from DNA sequence, advancing genomics research.
   - URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-024-02523-z

7. "Semantic design of functional de novo genes from a genomic language model" (Nature)
   - Demonstrates a genomic language model for designing functional new genes, pushing AI applications in synthetic biology and gene design.
   - URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09749-7

8. "A vision–language foundation model for precision oncology" (Nature)
   - Presents an AI model combining visual and textual data for precision cancer diagnosis and treatment recommendations.
   - URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-08378-w

9. "Efficient generation of protein pockets with PocketGen" (Nature Machine Intelligence)
   - Describes PocketGen, an AI tool for efficient design of protein binding pockets relevant for drug discovery.
   - URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-024-00920-9
